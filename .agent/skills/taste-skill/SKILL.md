# Taste Skill: Anti-Slop Frontend

Deliver premium visual craft and eliminate generic AI styling patterns.

## Core Directives

### 1. Eliminate Generic AI Tells
- **No Inter-by-default:** Pair characterful display typography with clean body fonts.
- **No generic purple gradients:** Avoid violet/cyan wash over saturated dark backgrounds. Use curated, intentional color ramps.
- **No cards-inside-cards:** Avoid nested bordered boxes. Use whitespace, subtle dividers, or surface elevation shifts.
- **No pure black/gray:** Dark neutrals must be tinted with subtle brand tones (cyan, slate, amber) rather than `#000000` or `#111111`.

### 2. Spacing & Rhythm
- Use deliberate, mathematical spacing scales (multiples of 4px / 8px).
- Give key elements room to breathe; maintain strong hierarchical focal points.

### 3. Repository Ground Truth
- Do not assume a `DESIGN-SYSTEM.md` or predefined design tokens exist. Check the actual frontend source/configuration and current demo brief first.
- The repository's first-demo plan leaves visual design open; choose a coherent palette, typography and layout that serve the finance workflow, document any tokens you add, and do not claim they are established project standards until committed and used.
