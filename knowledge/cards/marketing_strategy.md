# Marketing Economics & Business Strategy

## Customer Acquisition Cost by Channel
<!-- tools: tool_calculate_ltv_cac, tool_calculate_cac_payback | policy: unit_economics_policy -->
**What it is:** The total sales, ad spend, and marketing overhead required to acquire a paying customer, tracked separately by acquisition channel (paid search, social, direct sales, referrals).
**Why it matters:** Aggregated blended CAC often hides unprofitable channels that consume working capital without producing retained customers.
**Healthy vs warning:** Healthy: paid channels demonstrate predictable acquisition cost below 30% of first-year gross profit. Warning: rising channel CAC coupled with declining conversion velocity.
**What to do:** Reallocate marketing budget away from underperforming ad campaigns toward high-retention organic and referral channels.
**In the app:** Used in unit economics diagnostics and marketing runway planning.

## Blended vs Paid CAC
<!-- tools: tool_calculate_ltv_cac, tool_calculate_cac_payback | policy: unit_economics_policy -->
**What it is:** Paid CAC measures customer acquisition spend solely against paid marketing channels, while Blended CAC divides total marketing expenses across all customers including organic and word-of-mouth.
**Why it matters:** Relying on blended CAC can mask deteriorating paid unit economics and mislead leadership into overspending on unprofitable campaigns.
**Healthy vs warning:** Healthy: paid CAC remains within 1.5x of blended CAC. Warning: paid CAC escalates while organic referrals sustain the apparent blended average.
**What to do:** Measure channel-specific payback periods before scaling paid ad budgets.
**In the app:** Relevant when evaluating marketing spend efficiency and working capital allocation.

## Pricing Power and Contribution Margin
<!-- tools: tool_calculate_break_even, tool_calculate_break_even_units | policy: unit_economics_policy -->
**What it is:** An enterprise's ability to adjust product or service pricing upward without experiencing an unsustainable reduction in sales volume or customer retention.
**Why it matters:** Inflation and supplier price increases erode cash flow quickly unless an SME possesses sufficient pricing power to preserve gross margins.
**Healthy vs warning:** Healthy: ability to pass through 80% to 100% of cost increases while maintaining positive contribution margins. Warning: discounting prices to protect revenue volume while unit margin turns negative.
**What to do:** Conduct margin waterfalls by product line and eliminate negative-margin discounting.
**In the app:** Connected to break-even revenue and contribution margin calculations.

## Operating Leverage
<!-- tools: tool_calculate_fccr, tool_analyze_cash_flow | policy: debt_covenant_policy -->
**What it is:** The ratio of fixed operating overhead to variable operating costs, determining how sensitive operating income is to fluctuations in gross revenue.
**Why it matters:** High operating leverage accelerates profit growth during revenue expansions, but intensifies cash burn and insolvency risk during seasonal downturns.
**Healthy vs warning:** Healthy: fixed costs do not exceed 60% of baseline revenue during slow quarters. Warning: high fixed overhead coupled with fluctuating or seasonal top-line revenue.
**What to do:** Convert fixed overhead into variable costs through vendor outsourcing or flexible capacity agreements where possible.
**In the app:** Explored during fixed charge coverage (FCCR) and cash burn assessments.

## Sales Cycle Velocity and Working Capital
<!-- tools: tool_calculate_ccc, tool_calculate_wcr | policy: working_capital_policy -->
**What it is:** The elapsed time in days from first sales qualification to signed contract and cash collection, directly impacting the SME's cash conversion cycle.
**Why it matters:** A prolonged sales cycle traps cash in pre-deal operations and increases payroll burn before generating incoming receivables.
**Healthy vs warning:** Healthy: sales cycle duration is under 45 days for transactional accounts and under 90 days for mid-market accounts. Warning: lengthening deal cycles without staged milestone billings.
**What to do:** Require upfront deposits or milestone-based progress billings to fund operational costs during lengthy enterprise sales.
**In the app:** Supplements cash conversion cycle (CCC) and working capital requirement diagnostics.

## Interest Rate Sensitivity and Floating Debt
<!-- tools: tool_calculate_dscr, tool_calculate_debt_service_burden | policy: debt_covenant_policy -->
**What it is:** The vulnerability of an enterprise's cash flow to upward shifts in central bank benchmark rates on variable-rate credit lines and term loans.
**Why it matters:** Floating-rate facilities cause debt service payments to increase automatically, reducing net cash flow available for payroll and supplier obligations.
**Healthy vs warning:** Healthy: fixed-rate debt covers at least 70% of total liabilities, or DSCR exceeds 1.5x under a 200 basis point rate shock. Warning: unhedged variable lines operating near thin coverage covenants.
**What to do:** Lock in fixed rates or use surplus cash to retire high-cost variable debt when rate volatility increases.
**In the app:** Incorporated in debt service burden and prudent debt capacity analyses.
